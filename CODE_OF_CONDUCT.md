# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DTSpect welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participants
should be able to contribute without harassment or discrimination, regardless
of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity or orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate of different perspectives.
- Critique ideas and technical work without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and accept reasonable correction.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop unwelcome behaviour.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and retaliation against people raising concerns are
unacceptable. Repeated disruption and personal attacks are also unacceptable.

## Scope and responsibility

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed spaces, and when someone explicitly represents DTSpect in
public. The initial maintainer, [@Re-David-1992](https://github.com/Re-David-1992),
is responsible for enforcement.

## Raising a concern

For public content, you may flag a minimal, non-sensitive concern to the
maintainer in the relevant issue or PR, if you feel safe doing so. Do not post
private incident details or someone else's personal information. GitHub's
[abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
are also available for conduct on the platform.

**A dedicated confidential project reporting channel is not established yet.**
Use a private contact route only if the maintainer explicitly publishes one;
do not assume a GitHub profile provides private messaging. Establishing a
confidential reporting route is a community-foundation follow-up. This policy
does not promise a private team, response deadline, or independent appeals body
that does not exist.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will consider context and available evidence, respect privacy,
and explain decisions to affected participants where feasible. Responses may
include requesting a correction, removing inappropriate content, a warning,
temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban for serious or
repeated misconduct. Immediate action may be appropriate for threats or
disclosure of private information.

Participants may ask the maintainer to reconsider a decision and provide
additional context through an available safe contact route. Anyone involved
in a concern should disclose that conflict; until additional maintainers or
an independent route exist, the project cannot guarantee independent review.
Good-faith reporting and participation in a review must not result in retaliation.
